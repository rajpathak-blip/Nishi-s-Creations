# 🌸 Nishi's Creations - Designer Wear Boutique System

Custom local boutique system created for **Nishi's Creations (Designer Wear)** in Main Road Rangwasa, Rau, Indore.

---

## 🌟 What This System Does

### 1. 👗 Creations Vault (Media Dropzone & Catalog)
- **Drag & Drop** any photos or videos of your wife's stitching work:
  - 🥻 Blouses (Bridal, Maggam, Padded, Backless)
  - 👗 Lehenga Choli (16-kali, flare, festive)
  - 👧 Child / Kid Dresses & Gowns
  - 🪔 Bhagwan Poshak (Laddu Gopal, Radha Krishna, Mata Rani poshak)
  - 👘 Kurtis, Suits & Saree Alterations
- Organize with prices, fabric details, custom tags, and notes.

### 2. ✨ Social Post & Status Studio
- Select any product photo or upload any image on the fly.
- Switch formats in 1-click:
  - 📱 **WhatsApp Status & Instagram Reels / Story (9:16 - 1080x1920)**
  - 📷 **Instagram Post (1:1 Square - 1080x1080)**
  - 🖼️ **Instagram Portrait (4:5 Feed - 1080x1350)**
- **Luxury Watermark & Overlays**:
  - Golden "Nishi's Creations" Lotus & Needle Logo
  - Contact Details: `📞 6260808658` • `📸 @NISHIS_CREATIONS`
  - Address: `Main Road Rangwasa, Rau, Indore`
  - Customizable price badge (e.g. `₹1800` or `Custom Stitched`)
  - Royal gold double-border & vignette frame
- **Instant High-Res PNG Download** (1080p).
- **Auto-generated Captions & Hashtags** with 1-click **Copy Caption** and **Share to WhatsApp** buttons!

### 3. 🧾 Boutique Billing & PDF Invoices
- Quick-add common boutique tailoring items (Bridal Blouse, Maggam work, Lehenga stitching, Fall & Pico, etc.).
- Auto calculations for Subtotal, Discount, Advance Received, and Balance Remaining.
- Measurement cheat-sheet for blouse, lehenga, and kids dresses.
- **Export to PDF**: Produces a clean, luxury fashion house style invoice with logo, item breakdown, and terms.
- **WhatsApp Bill Sender**: 1-click button that opens WhatsApp with customer phone number and a friendly formatted message detailing their bill, advance paid, balance due, and promised delivery date!

### 4. 📋 Customer Order Ledger
- Real-time tracking of order statuses (`In Stitching`, `Ready for Trial`, `Delivered`).
- 1-click WhatsApp customer ping: *"Namaste! Your outfit is ready for trial..."*
- Quick business overview: Total orders, active stitching count, advance received, and pending collections.
